# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Soroban-Profiler to be a welcoming project for anyone building on Soroban, regardless of experience level. We pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, PRs, and reviews.
- Assume good faith — most disagreements are about tradeoffs, not people.
- Give specific, actionable feedback on code rather than general criticism.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other official communication channels for the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior by emailing the maintainer listed on the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/CollinsKRO). Reports will be reviewed and addressed promptly, with confidentiality respected. Maintainers may remove comments, close PRs, or ban contributors for violations.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
